# Wordloom Product Constitution

Status: Canonical Studio profile memory

Scope: This is a concise product decision guardrail for the Wordloom profile. It is not
a roadmap, legal assurance, policy page, or feature backlog.

## Purpose

Wordloom is an automated, low-work-for-teachers spelling intervention system. Product
decisions should help teachers understand the current learning situation, oversee the
next useful support, and keep pupil-facing spelling work clear, calm, and purposeful.

## Principles

1. Automation before unnecessary teacher administration. Prefer policy-driven support,
   Support Ladder delivery, analytics insight, and intervention oversight before adding
   recurring teacher setup or manual test-management work.
2. Simplicity before feature density. Keep teacher-facing choices limited and named
   clearly; do not expose internal mechanics, specialist controls, or manual builder
   paths as the normal product experience.
3. Progressive disclosure before clutter. Show the current situation, blockers, and
   next action first; reveal advanced, legacy, or recovery tools only when they are
   relevant.
4. Pupil clarity and accessibility. Use age-appropriate wording, clear instructions,
   understandable feedback, usable controls, and cautious accessibility claims backed by
   current evidence.
5. Educational purpose before gamification. Games, competitions, rewards, and novelty
   should serve spelling practice, confidence, evidence, or review rather than add noise
   or status pressure.
6. Deterministic and evidence-led assessment. Spelling correctness should be
   deterministic where appropriate. Analytics, AI support, and attainment indicators are
   review aids, not diagnoses, fixed labels, or substitutes for professional judgement.
7. Support Ladder by default where appropriate. Distinguish independent success, retry,
   supported success, supported difficulty, and access issues so support evidence helps
   teaching without overstating mastery.
8. Visibility is not permission. School visibility, read-only access, analytics
   visibility, edit rights, assignment rights, and sharing permissions are separate
   product concepts.
9. Data minimisation by purpose. Do not collect, store, or use pupil or school data
   without a defined and documented product, educational, operational, legal, or safety
   purpose. Use the minimum information needed and avoid unnecessary identifiable or
   sensitive data.
10. Human oversight for consequential educational logic. Metrics, generated suggestions,
   and analytics may surface evidence or prompt review, but should not autonomously make
   consequential educational decisions merely because measured values change.
11. Quality and consistency before isolated convenience. Preserve school scoping,
   deterministic behaviour, cautious public/compliance wording, test evidence, and
   coherent product vocabulary. Report conflicts in existing guidance rather than
   silently choosing.

## Context Discipline

Use this Constitution when a Wordloom product decision needs durable product memory.
Do not automatically inject the full text into every Studio or agent prompt; future
prompt selection should reference it only when the task needs Wordloom product policy.
